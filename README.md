🏦 Advance Bank Term Deposit Analysis
📌 Introduction

The Advance Bank Term Deposit project focuses on analyzing customer data to understand the factors that influence a customer's decision to subscribe to a term deposit.

The project uses Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA), data visualization, statistical analysis, and Logistic Regression to identify important customer characteristics and campaign-related factors that affect term deposit subscriptions.

The goal is to transform raw banking data into meaningful business insights that can help banks improve their marketing campaigns, customer targeting, and subscription conversion rates.

🎯 Project Objective

The main objectives of this project are:

Analyze customer demographics and financial information.
Identify customer groups more likely to subscribe to term deposits.
Understand the impact of job, age, balance, and contact method.
Analyze the effectiveness of marketing campaigns.
Identify important factors influencing subscription decisions.
Build a Logistic Regression model to predict term deposit subscription.
📊 Key Analysis

The project analyzes several important factors, including:

👤 Age
💼 Job
💰 Account Balance
📞 Contact Method
📅 Campaign Duration
🔁 Number of Contacts
🏦 Term Deposit Subscription
🔍 Key Takeaways
Certain age groups and job categories are more likely to subscribe to term deposits.
Customers with higher account balances generally show a higher tendency toward subscription.
Cellular communication performs better than traditional telephone contact.
The number of contacts and campaign duration can significantly influence campaign outcomes.
Logistic Regression helps identify the most important features associated with term deposit subscription.

🤖 Machine Learning

A Logistic Regression model is used to predict whether a customer is likely to subscribe to a term deposit.

Why Logistic Regression?

Logistic Regression is suitable because the target variable represents a binary outcome:

Yes → Customer subscribed
No → Customer did not subscribe

The model also helps understand which features have a stronger relationship with the subscription outcome.
